cardenolide 强心苷、[强心内酯](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardenolide), e.g. 地高辛

Apocynaceae plants [夹竹桃科 - 维基百科](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apocynaceae)

monarch butterflies 帝王斑蝶

Na+,K+-ATPase, ATPα

拮抗多效性（*antagonistic pleiotropy*）negative pleiotropy? a single gene or genetic variant affects multiple traits or characteristics in an organism, but these effects are in opposite or conflicting directions.

parallel evolution: different species face similar pressures and develop similar traits as a result.

orthologs are defined as homologs in different species that catalyze the same reaction, and paralogs are defined as homologs in the same species that do not catalyze the same reaction.
